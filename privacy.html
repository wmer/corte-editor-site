Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Corte-Editor is an internal tool that edits clips and publishes them to the YouTube channels operated by its owner (Alta Linguagem [Cortes] and PodCuts). It is not offered to the public; its only user is the owner of those channels. Using Corte-Editor requires agreeing to this Privacy Policy.

YouTube API Services

Corte-Editor uses YouTube API Services. By using Corte-Editor you are also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Data we access

Only after the channel owner signs in with Google and grants access, Corte-Editor accesses:

  • the channel's identity (channel ID and name);
  • the channel's uploaded videos and their metadata: title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy and publishing schedule, processing status and original file name;
  • statistics of the channel's own videos (views, likes and comments);
  • YouTube Analytics and Reporting data of the channel: views, engaged views, watch time, subscribers, audience retention, impressions and click-through rate;
  • comments posted by the channel itself on its own videos.

Permissions requested: youtube, youtube.force-ssl, youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly.

Corte-Editor may also read limited public information about a third-party YouTube video (such as its title, channel and link) when a clip comes from it, in order to prepare the clip and credit the full video.

How we use the data

  • to upload videos to the owner's channels and keep their title, description, tags, thumbnail and schedule up to date;
  • to post the channel's own comment on its videos without duplicating it;
  • to measure how published clips perform and decide which topics and formats to produce next;
  • to suggest new titles, thumbnails and tags, which are applied only after the owner approves each one.

How we share the data

We do not sell YouTube data and do not share it with advertisers or other third parties. To generate analyses and suggestions, the titles, descriptions, transcripts and performance numbers of the channel's own videos may be sent to AI model providers (such as OpenRouter, Google and Abacus.AI), which process them under their own terms. No Google account credentials are ever sent to them.

Storage and retention

  • All data is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local database and local files. Corte-Editor has no server that stores YouTube data.
  • Google access tokens are stored locally and are never shared.
  • Data about the channel's own videos is refreshed at least every 30 days and deleted when the video is removed from the channel.
  • Analytics and reporting data is kept to compare performance over time, and Corte-Editor confirms that it is still authorized to access it at least every 30 days.
  • Public data about third-party videos is not stored for more than 30 days unless it is refreshed.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke Corte-Editor's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page. After access is revoked, Corte-Editor stops accessing YouTube data and deletes the stored data obtained with that authorization within 7 calendar days.

To ask for your data to be deleted, contact us through the channel below. We delete it as soon as possible and within 7 calendar days.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers.
