Corte-Editor

An internal clip-editing and publishing tool for our own YouTube channels.

Corte-Editor turns long live streams into short clips and highlights, and publishes them to the YouTube channels operated by its owner:

  • Alta Linguagem [Cortes]
  • PodCuts

How it uses YouTube

With the channel owner's authorization (Google OAuth), Corte-Editor uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs to:

  • upload videos and set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and publishing schedule;
  • post the channel's own comment on its videos;
  • read the channel's own video list, statistics and analytics to learn which topics and formats work best.

Corte-Editor is not offered to the public: it runs on its owner's computer and its only user is the owner of the channels above.
